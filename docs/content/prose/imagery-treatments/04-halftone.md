Reserve the dot treatment for Standard. It signals the product family, so applying it to RoleModel or client photography dilutes that. Use it on process and workspace shots, never on faces — dithering flattens skin and reads as a printing error. Apply it live rather than flattening it into the source file, and keep the originals: the treatment is a rendering, not an edit.
